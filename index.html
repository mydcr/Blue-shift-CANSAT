MISSION 01PRIMARY
⌁

Required mission

BlueShift will launch a CanSat weighing under 1 kg to approximately 1,000 m. It will demonstrate gyroscopic descent control and a ground-controlled parachute opening at 600 m, while measuring air temperature and pressure and transmitting data to the ground station every second. The CanSat must remain intact throughout the mission.
